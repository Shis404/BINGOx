🎲 How to Play BINGO 25

1

Arrange Numbers 1 to 25

Shuffle randomly, or clear the grid and tap boxes in order to place 1–25 anywhere you like. Tap any two numbers to swap them!

2

Take Turns — 1 Cross Each

In Online or 2-Player mode, players alternate crossing 1 number per turn. Your grids stay hidden online, and every cut automatically syncs to both sheets!

3

Complete 5 Lines to Spell B-I-N-G-O

Every completed horizontal row, vertical column, or diagonal unlocks the next letter: B → I → N → G → O. First to 5 lines wins!
